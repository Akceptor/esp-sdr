ESP-SDR in the browser

ESP-SDR turns an ESP32 into a small 2.4 GHz software-defined radio that streams raw I/Q snapshots over a serial port. These pages talk to it directly from your browser. Nothing is uploaded; everything runs locally.

ELRS stick viewer

Connect an ESP32 running ESP-SDR over Web Serial, enter your ExpressLRS binding phrase, and watch the transmitter sticks and AUX channels live. Decodes ELRS DK500 (LR1121, 2.4 GHz GFSK) packets in JavaScript.

Open viewer

Web flasher

Flash the official ESP-SDR firmware to an ESP32 (WROOM-32 / WROOM-32U) from Chrome or Edge, no tools to install.

Open flasher

Requirements

A desktop Chromium-based browser (Chrome, Edge, Opera) with Web Serial support, served over HTTPS or localhost. Firefox and Safari do not implement Web Serial.

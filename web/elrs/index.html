MHz · ±8 MHz around 2436 MHz; only channels in this span are visible. Magenta = decoded packet.

Mode 2 layout, CRSF values 172..1811. AUX1 is the arm bit. With Hybrid8 switches each AUX updates only when its slot comes round, so a bar can lag a few packets. The phrase is hashed in your browser and never sent anywhere. The receiver is tuned to 2436 MHz and sees about 12 of the 80 hopping channels, so only a fraction of snapshots contain a packet.

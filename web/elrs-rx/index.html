AUX

Spectrum

MHz · ±8 MHz around the receiver LO; only channels in this span are visible. Magenta = decoded packet.

Mode 2 layout, CRSF values 172..1811. AUX1 is the arm bit. With Hybrid8 switches each AUX updates only when its slot comes round, so a bar can lag a few packets. Decoding runs on the board; the phrase is hashed in your browser and only the resulting UID is sent to the board.

Viewer only. Not flight-safe. The receiver never transmits.
